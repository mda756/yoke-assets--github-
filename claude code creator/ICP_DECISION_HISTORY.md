## 📅 SESSION 2: 2026-02-04 - MEDICAL SOCIETIES HYPOTHESIS VALIDATION

### Context:
User chose option 3: Research Medical Societies to validate the 10/10 hypothesis before making final ICP decision. Conducted web research on UK CPD requirements, accreditation standards, pain points, and current tools.

### Validation Results:

#### ✅ REGULATORY PRESSURE CONFIRMED

**GMC Revalidation Requirements:**
- 50 CPD hours per year minimum
- 250 CPD credits over 5-year revalidation cycle
- Must include reflection on learning and impact
- Certificates of attendance NOT sufficient - need evidence of learning outcomes

**Royal College Standards:**
- Must participate in complete audit cycle every 5 years
- Recording of CPD must include "reflection of the learning gained and likely effect on professional work"
- "Certificates of attendance prove attendance but say nothing about what has been learned"
- Analysis of outcomes required for revalidation

**Sources:**
- [GMC CPD Guidance](https://www.gmc-uk.org/education/standards-guidance-and-curricula/guidance/continuing-professional-development)
- [Royal College of Surgeons CPD](https://www.rcseng.ac.uk/standards-and-research/standards-and-guidance/revalidation/cpd/)
- [RCOG CPD Requirements](https://www.rcog.org.uk/careers-and-training/cpd-revalidation/the-cpd-framework/cpd-framework-faqs/cpd-faqs-cpd-categories-and-activities/)

---

#### ✅ PAIN/FAILURE MOMENT CONFIRMED

**Current Challenge (from research):**
> "Measuring the impact of CPD activity on patient outcomes is acknowledged as difficult... practitioners must try to identify ways in which their CPD activities could help to improve the quality of care provided."

**Evidence Gap:**
> "The majority of accredited continuing professional development activities do not target clinical behavior change"

> "Evidence linking CPD directly to patient outcomes remains limited and methodologically diverse"

**Accreditation Requirements:**
- IACPDA international standards require "outcome assessment of educational activity"
- ACCME (USA model followed by UK) stipulates "CME providers should evaluate their programs using outcome measures to obtain accreditation"
- Pre/post-test assessments for knowledge gain
- OSCEs for skill acquisition
- Clinical audits for patient outcomes

**Sources:**
- [CPD Outcomes Research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12492535/)
- [CPD Best Practices](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4005177/)
- [Academy Standards](https://www.aomrc.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Accreditation_standards_0719.pdf)

**Pain Validated:** Societies need to prove outcomes but current tools only track completion.

---

#### ✅ COMPETITOR GAP CONFIRMED

**Current Tools Used by Societies:**
- BMJ Learning (evidence-based courses, CPD-accredited content)
- RCGP Learning (clinical modules for GPs)
- MIMS Learning (quizzes, webinars, articles)
- Lumis LMS (over 150 CPD-certified e-learning courses)
- LearnPro (used across NHS Scotland for mandatory learning)
- Royal College online CPD Diaries (self-recording)

**What These Tools Do:**
- Track course completions
- Issue certificates of attendance
- Allow manual reflection notes
- Monitor credit accumulation

**What They DON'T Do:**
- Measure behavior change
- Track clinical decision impact
- Show confidence shifts over time
- Demonstrate patient outcomes
- Provide automated outcomes evidence for accreditation audits

**Sources:**
- [BMJ Learning](https://www.learninglight.com/best-lms-for-healthcare/)
- [Lumis LMS](https://mylearningcloud.org.uk/learning-management-system/)
- [LearnPro](https://learnpro.co.uk/products/lms/learning-management-system)

**Gap Confirmed:** Existing LMS platforms focus on completion tracking, not outcomes measurement.

---

**Quarterly/Annual Events:**

   - Medscape case study = outcomes measurement credibility
